Personal research · Oct 2026

I tested my own instincts with 55 videos.

I believed humor and short videos always win. My longest video, 2 minutes 38 seconds, crossed 859K organic views anyway. So I analyzed 55 of my sponsored videos in Python with Mann-Whitney U tests, Spearman correlations and regression with robustness checks.

  • →Humor-first videos earned 2.8× the median views (p = 0.0001).
  • →But in 45 of 46 humor-first videos, the average viewer left before the brand appeared.
  • →Longer videos did not perform worse.
